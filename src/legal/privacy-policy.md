# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 27, 2026*

VetTV Handheld News ("we," "us") is published by VetTV Studios. This page explains what information the site collects and how it's used.

## What we collect
- **Nothing while you read.** The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics, ad trackers or third-party tracking scripts.
- **Newsletter signups.** If you subscribe, your email address goes to our newsletter provider, [Buttondown](https://buttondown.com/legal/privacy), which stores it and sends the newsletter. We use it only to send that newsletter.
- **Email you send us.** If you write to us, we keep your message and address only to reply and follow up.

## Hosting
The site is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log basic technical data, such as IP addresses, to run and secure its service, under [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Links and embeds
Articles link to other websites. Their privacy practices are their own.

## Your choices
You can unsubscribe from the newsletter with the link in any issue. To ask us to delete your email or messages, write to [Vetgaming1982@outlook.com](mailto:Vetgaming1982@outlook.com).

## Children
The site isn't aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

## Changes
If this policy changes, we'll update the date above.
