# Content Disclosure

*Last updated: October 27, 2026*

## How we report
- We verify facts, quotes and numbers and link our sources in each article.
- When we can't confirm a claim, we say so.
- News and opinion are kept separate, and analysis and opinion pieces are labeled.
- Unofficial tools, mods and leaks are identified as unofficial.

## Trackers
The Upcoming, Soft Launches and Delistings trackers list each entry's source and the date we last checked it. Unconfirmed dates are shown as TBA. Dates can change, so check the source before you rely on one.

## Our own projects
VetTV develops its own software, including VetTV Retro OS, a fork of ReactOS that is still in development. Articles that touch on our projects or close competitors carry a disclosure.

## Tools we use
VetTV uses AI-assisted tools for research and drafting. Every article is reviewed and approved by Vet before it's published.

## Corrections
If we get something wrong, we fix it and note the correction in the article. Send corrections to [Vetgaming1982@outlook.com](mailto:Vetgaming1982@outlook.com).
