The biggest PC story this week was [Advanced Shader Delivery reaching every major GPU vendor](/vettv-handheld-news/posts/advanced-shader-delivery-all-rtx-2026-10/). We've covered it separately. Here's everything else.

## ID@Xbox x IGN Showcase (Oct. 20)

The [ID@Xbox and IGN showcase](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/10/20/id-xbox-ign-showcase-october-2026/) included the reveals of *A Game About Digging a Hole Together* (Dec. 4, Game Pass) and *Magenta Down* (early 2027). Three CRITICAL REFLEX games are coming to Xbox:
- **Arctic Eggs** launched on Xbox Series X|S the same day as the show.
- **Mouthwashing** comes to Series X|S, Xbox on PC, Xbox One and Game Pass on **Oct. 29**.
- **No, I'm Not a Human** arrives in 2027.

**Shrek 2 Remastered** launches on Series X|S on **Nov. 10**. Pre-orders are up to 20% off with Game Pass and 10% off without it, through Nov. 9. **ZERO PARADES: Director's Cut** arrives Nov. 3. **Scramble Knights Online** launched today, Oct. 26, as a Game Pass Game Preview.

## Next week on Xbox (Oct. 26-30)

Xbox Wire's [weekly list](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/10/23/next-week-on-xbox-new-games-for-october-26-to-30/) has roughly 50 games. **The Wolf Among Us Remastered** arrives Oct. 29 on Series X|S and Xbox One. It adds a Noir Mode and restored deleted scenes. **Mouthwashing** ($12.99, Play Anywhere) launches the same day. Other highlights are *The Séance of Blake Manor* (Oct. 27, Game Pass), *Terrible Lizards* (Oct. 29, Game Pass), *SULFUR* (Oct. 28) and *Croc 2* (Oct. 29). Release dates can still change.

## Game Pass in a phone plan (Oct. 26)

T-Mobile announced T-Mix, which lets U.S. customers choose the perks in their wireless plan. Starting **in November**, [eligible T-Mix customers can choose Game Pass Ultimate or Premium](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/10/26/xbox-game-pass-t-mobile/). Xbox says this is the first time Game Pass has been offered through a U.S. wireless plan. Eligibility details are on T-Mobile's side.

## Windows Insider builds (Oct. 23)

[Experimental build 26340.9767](https://blogs.windows.com/windows-insider/2026/10/23/announcing-new-builds-for-23-october-2026/) adds a **speed test in Quick Settings** and a **Mobile devices panel in Quick Settings**. The panel has the fullest controls for Android phones (Do Not Disturb, ringer mode and play a sound), while iPhone gets a more limited status view. Feature flags in Insider settings can now be filtered by enablement state. There are no gaming changes. Beta build 26220.9757 also came out the same day.

## Xbox PC app on Arm: not new this week

Some download sites list Xbox app **2610.1.1** (dated Oct. 22) with "now available on Arm-based Windows 11 PCs" release notes. That announcement is actually from **[Jan. 21, 2026](https://blogs.windows.com/windowsexperience/2026/01/21/play-more-xbox-app-is-now-available-on-arm-based-windows-11-pcs/)**. Microsoft said then that more than 85% of the Game Pass catalog works on Arm PCs. 2610.1.1 looks like a routine app update, and we couldn't find official notes saying it does anything new for Arm.

## Arkane Lyon gets one more month

Microsoft has extended the consultation over Arkane Lyon's future by one month. A representative told [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/gaming-industry/time-could-be-running-out-for-arkane/) that the company is exploring options, "including finding a partner to support the studio through the development work still ahead." That work is Marvel's Blade. Microsoft called it "a defined extension." Reports, including [Push Square's](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2026/10/microsoft-gives-dishonored-dev-more-time-to-find-a-buyer-needs-a-partner-to-finish-blade), put the new end date at **Dec. 13**, a month after the previously reported **Nov. 13**. Microsoft's quoted statement mentions a partner, not a buyer. Any sale is still speculation.

*Opinion:* A one-month extension is good news for the Dishonored team. But looking for a partner to finish Blade is different from saving the studio long term, and Dec. 13 is close to the holidays.
