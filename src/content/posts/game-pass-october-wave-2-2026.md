Xbox Wire's [October Wave 2 post](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/10/20/xbox-game-pass-october-2026-wave-2/) went up Oct. 20, and the [@XBOXGamePass account](https://x.com/XBOXGamePass/status/2112574572012675169) shared it the same day. It covers nine additions through Nov. 3, an ESO update, two in-game perks, and 11 games leaving soon. Some of these, including Mouthwashing and Scramble Knights Online, were in our [weekly roundup](/vettv-handheld-news/posts/windows-xbox-week-2026-10-26/). This piece adds what that one didn't cover: **which tier gets each game** and the full list of departures.

## Coming to Game Pass

| Date | Game | Platforms | Tiers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oct. 21 | Sandustry (Game Preview) | PC | Now in Premium; was already in Ultimate and PC Game Pass |
| Oct. 22 | Dodo Duckie | Cloud, Series X/S, PC | Ultimate, Premium, PC Game Pass |
| Oct. 26 | Scramble Knights Online (Game Preview), day one | Cloud, Series X/S, PC | Ultimate, PC Game Pass |
| Oct. 27 | The Séance of Blake Manor | Cloud, Series X/S, PC | Ultimate, Premium, PC Game Pass |
| Oct. 29 | Final Fantasy VII Remake Intergrade | Cloud, Series X/S, PC | Ultimate, Premium, PC Game Pass |
| Oct. 29 | Mouthwashing | Cloud, Series X/S, Handheld, PC | Ultimate, Premium, PC Game Pass |
| Oct. 29 | Plants vs. Zombies: Replanted | Cloud, Console, PC | Ultimate, PC Game Pass |
| Oct. 29 | Terrible Lizards, day one | Cloud, Series X/S, PC | Ultimate, PC Game Pass |
| Nov. 3 | Pit of Goblin | Cloud, Series X/S, PC | Ultimate, Premium, PC Game Pass |

**The big one is Final Fantasy VII Remake Intergrade.** It's coming to Premium as well as Ultimate and PC Game Pass. **Mouthwashing** was added to the post later the same day, after the [ID@Xbox showcase](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/10/20/id-xbox-ign-showcase-october-2026/) announced it. It's the only game on the list labeled "Handheld," which Xbox says marks games optimized for handheld play. Plants vs. Zombies: Replanted is described as part of EA Play.

*Tier note:* Scramble Knights Online, Plants vs. Zombies: Replanted and Terrible Lizards are listed only for **Ultimate and PC Game Pass**, not Premium.

## Updates and perks

- **The Elder Scrolls Online: Season 2 Update, "Echoes of Skyrim"** (Oct. 21): adds Western Skyrim, two solo dungeons, and the Season Two Tamriel Tome, available until Jan. 27.
- **The Finals Welcome Pack** (available now): 500 Multibucks, a 25% bonus XP boost and the "Pretty Hostile" set. It's for Ultimate, Premium, Essential and PC Game Pass, and only while the current season lasts.
- **World of Tanks Modern Armor XP Stock** (Nov. 3): 20 each of Enhanced Repair Kits, Enhanced Med Kits and Automatic Fire Suppressors. Same four tiers.

## Leaving Oct. 31

All nine are on Cloud, Console and PC: Day of the Tentacle Remastered, Dead Static Drive, Deep Rock Galactic, Full Throttle Remastered, Grim Fandango Remastered, Lara Croft and the Temple of Osiris, LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga, Wartales and Wildgate. Xbox says subscribers can buy these at a discount before they leave. It didn't list the prices.

**Leaving Nov. 10:** Football Manager 26 (PC) and Football Manager 26 – Console Edition.
