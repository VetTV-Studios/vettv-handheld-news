Saints Row: The Third Remastered launched on iOS and Android on Thursday, October 29, 2026. Feral Interactive handled the port, and it's a premium release at $19.99 / £12.99 / €15.99, with no free-to-play model.

## What's in the package

It's the 2020 remaster of Volition's 2011 open-world crime game, in which the Third Street Saints try to take the city of Steelport from the Syndicate. Feral says the mobile version keeps the enhanced visuals from the console release. All the original DLC is included at no extra cost, including Genkibowl VII, Gangstas in Space and The Trouble With Clones, plus more than 30 extra weapons, vehicles and costumes. You can play the whole campaign solo or in online co-op with a friend.

## Controls

The game has fully customizable touch controls. Gamepads work on every supported device, and keyboard and mouse work on iPadOS and Android.

## Requirements

- **iOS / iPadOS:** iOS 18 or later and 22.12GB of free space. Feral recommends leaving about twice that free for the install. Supported iPhones are the XS and XS Max, the iPhone 11 series and later, the iPhone SE (3rd gen) and the iPhone Air. Supported iPads are the iPad 10th gen and later, the iPad mini 6th gen and later, the iPad Air 4th gen and later, and the iPad Pro 3rd gen (2018) and later. It's rated 17+ on the App Store.
- **Android:** Android 13 or later. Feral says Google Play blocks purchases on devices that can't run the game, though some unsupported devices may slip through.
