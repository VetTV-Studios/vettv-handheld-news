## Titanfall 2's VR mod opens a multiplayer beta

CircuitLord's free, unofficial Titanfall 2 VR mod, which brought the campaign to VR late last month, now has an open multiplayer beta with North American and European servers, Road to VR reports. The mod reworks the game's controls for VR, including interactive reloading for every gun and VR versions of wall-running, sliding and the grappling hook. You need your own copy of Titanfall 2, a VR-ready PC and a PC VR headset that supports OpenXR. It installs through CircuitLord's mod installer.

## Legion Go S with SteamOS: $949.99 (price as of Oct 29, may change)

Lenovo is selling the SteamOS version of the Legion Go S with a Ryzen Z1 Extreme, 32GB of RAM and a 1TB SSD for $949.99 shipped with promo code DEALDAYSLIVE, according to 9to5Toys on October 29. That outlet says this configuration launched at $900, rose to $1,580 after recent price hikes, and dropped to $1,000 at Best Buy earlier this month. It has an 8-inch 120Hz display with VRR. Promo pricing can end at any time, so check the price before buying.

## PowerWash Simulator on Apple Arcade adds aquatic jobs and Quick Play

FuturLab released an update to PowerWash Simulator on Apple Arcade on October 29. It adds two Muckingham Files jobs, the Aquarium and the Submarine, and brings back the Halloween House seasonal job. A new Quick Play mode lets you pick a level size, choose from three random jobs or reroll, and play with the best equipment and unlimited cleaning liquid. Jobs finished in Quick Play count toward Career Mode. The update also includes bug fixes and quality-of-life improvements.

## Monster Hunter Outlanders launches on iOS and Android

TiMi Studio Group's Monster Hunter Outlanders, developed under license from Capcom, launched on iOS and Android on October 29. It launches with eight weapon types, including the newly added Charge Blade, plus four-player co-op, three kinds of Buddies and the original Elder Dragon Ruger Aidal. Controller support at launch covers hunts, the quest system and world exploration, and TiMi says it will expand support to more of the game in future updates. A mainland China version is planned for 2027 or later.
