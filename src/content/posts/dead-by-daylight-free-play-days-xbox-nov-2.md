Dead by Daylight is free to play for all Xbox users through Monday, November 2, as part of this weekend's Free Play Days. You don't need Game Pass to play it.

## Keeping it

If you want to keep playing after the weekend, Dead by Daylight costs $11.99 to buy, according to GAMINGbible.

## The rest of Free Play Days

Four more games are part of Free Play Days this weekend, but they require Game Pass:

- Wuchang
- Spirittea
- Darkwood
- Everspace 2
