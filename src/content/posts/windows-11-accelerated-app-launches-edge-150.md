Windows 11 has a new feature called accelerated app launches, and Microsoft Edge 150 is the first app to use it.

## What it does

According to Microsoft, accelerated app launches make reopening an app that's already running between 15% and 33% faster. Edge 150 is the first app built to take advantage of it.

## What you need

The feature requires Windows 11's October preview update, KB5127936, or a later update. We covered what else that update brings in our [KB5127936 story](/vettv-handheld-news/posts/windows-11-kb5127936-context-menu-xbox-task-view/).

## Other apps

Apps don't get the speedup automatically. Developers have to opt in through a limited-access API, which Microsoft documents on Microsoft Learn.
