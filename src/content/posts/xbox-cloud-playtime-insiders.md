Xbox Insiders who don't have an active Game Pass subscription can now buy **cloud playtime** to stream games, [Xbox Wire announced on October 21](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/10/21/cloud-playtime-now-available-to-test-with-xbox-insiders/). Xbox calls it a preview ahead of a broader rollout "in November."

## Pricing

Xbox lists these as "introductory launch pricing" for Insiders:

- **10 hours for $3.99**
- **25 hours for $7.99**
- **50 hours for $11.99**

## What you get

According to Xbox, the packs give an **ad-free** cloud gaming experience with shorter wait times, high-quality streaming, and playtime "that remains available until it's used." Xbox doesn't describe the hours as expiring.

The fine print matters. Xbox says cloud playtime "requires cloud playable game purchase or for select-games, free-to-play access," so it streams **games you own** that support cloud play, plus selected free-to-play titles. It isn't a Game Pass library. Availability, eligible games and features "may vary by market, device, account eligibility, and preview ring," and a supported device is required.

Xbox also notes that Insiders can already try cloud gaming **free with ads**, so paid playtime sits between the ad-supported option and Game Pass.

## Our take

Hour packs are a sensible middle ground for people who own a few cloud-playable games and only stream now and then. The real test is the November rollout: which markets get it, and whether "introductory" pricing goes up afterwards.
