Valve released a **[Half-Life: Alyx update](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1847286114046999)** on **October 26, 2026** (posted at 5:49 PM CT). It has four changes, and two of them are about getting more performance out of VR headsets.

## Foveated rendering on desktop

There's a new setting under **Advanced Performance** that turns on **foveated rendering on desktop** for supported hardware. Foveated rendering draws full detail where you're looking and less in your peripheral vision. Valve says it "can increase rendering performance." It doesn't give any numbers.

- **DirectX:** NVIDIA **GTX 16xx and newer** or **RTX 20xx and newer**.
- **AMD:** Launch the game with the **-vulkan** option.
- **Eye tracking:** Valve says to turn on eye tracking on your headset for best results. It links to a [Steam Community guide](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3822057317) with more details.

## Steam Frame reprojection

When the game runs **natively on Steam Frame**, it now uses **stencil buffer tagging** to help improve reprojection quality. Reprojection fills in frames when the game can't hit the headset's refresh rate, so better reprojection should mean fewer visual artifacts when performance dips.

## Fixes

- **Workshop:** Several rendering crashes common in workshop content are fixed.
- **Achievement:** "Gnome Alone" no longer fails to unlock for some users.
