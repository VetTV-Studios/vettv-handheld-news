Achievements for some **original Xbox** games are appearing on Xbox Series X|S players' profiles before Microsoft has announced their rollout, [Windows Central reports](https://www.windowscentral.com/gaming/xbox/microsoft-hasnt-announced-og-xbox-achievements-yet-but-xbox-consoles-may-have-spoiled-the-surprise).

Microsoft announced in July 2026, via Xbox Wire, that original Xbox games would get achievements on modern consoles and PC. What hasn't been announced is these particular games going live.

## What's been spotted

Photos shared by X users @xgramiliki and @PiquenoLoredo (via @CentralXBOX_) show achievement entries for:

- Destroy All Humans!
- Full Spectrum Warrior
- Star Wars: Republic Commando
- Panzer Dragoon Orta
- KOF: Neowave

Each shows **"0/0"** achievements and no Gamerscore, which suggests the lists went up before they were ready. Windows Central speculates they may be pulled and restored later.

## Not retroactive

Windows Central notes that Xbox VP Jason Ronald has confirmed OG Xbox achievements **won't unlock retroactively**, so you'll need to play the games again to earn them.

## Our take

A 0/0 listing is a placeholder, not a launch. Until Xbox confirms dates and the game list, treat this as a sign that the July promise is getting close.
