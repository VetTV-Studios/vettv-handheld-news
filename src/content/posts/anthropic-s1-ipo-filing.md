**Anthropic**, the company behind Claude, has filed to go public. Its [announcement](https://www.anthropic.com/news/s1-sec) says that on **October 26, 2026**, Anthropic, PBC filed a registration statement on Form S-1 with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission for a proposed initial public offering of its Class A common stock.

We confirmed the filing on **SEC EDGAR**: an [S-1 from Anthropic, PBC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026067168/0001628280-26-067168-index.htm) (CIK 0002130357), filed October 26, 2026, file number 333-299633.

## What's set and what isn't

- Anthropic plans to list on the **Nasdaq Global Select Market** under the symbol **ANT**.
- The **number of shares and the price range haven't been set**.
- The registration statement **isn't effective yet**, so no shares can be sold until it is.
- Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley are named as the contacts for the preliminary prospectus.

## Numbers from the filing

These figures come from the [S-1 itself](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026067168/anthropicpbc-sx1.htm):

- **Revenue:** $16.3 billion for the six months ended June 30, 2026, up from $1.2 billion a year earlier. Full-year revenue was $4.6 billion in 2025.
- **Operating loss:** $693 million for the first half of 2026, down from $2.1 billion a year earlier.
- **Net loss:** $80.7 billion for the first half of 2026. The filing attributes most of that to a $72.4 billion change in the fair value of convertible notes, derivative liabilities and SAFEs, not to day-to-day operations.

The prospectus also describes Anthropic's governance as a public benefit corporation. Its Long-Term Benefit Trust holds a special Class T stock that can elect a majority of the board. A single Class F share, held by an LLC of the co-founders, would carry 50.1% of the vote on general stockholder matters.

The preliminary prospectus is incomplete and can change before the offering. Nothing here is investment advice.
