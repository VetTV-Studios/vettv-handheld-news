Here are the highlights of next week's Xbox releases, from Mezha's roundup of Xbox Wire's weekly list.

## ZERO PARADES: Director's Cut

ZERO PARADES: Director's Cut, from the team behind Disco Elysium, launches on November 3. It supports Xbox Play Anywhere.

## Football Manager 27

Football Manager 27's early access on PC opens November 3. You can get in by preordering the game or through Game Pass.

## Game Pass launches

These four games launch into Game Pass next week:

- GRAVHOUNDS
- Pit of Goblin
- VOID/BREAKER
- Escape Academy 2
