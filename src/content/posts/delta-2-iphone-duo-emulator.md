**Delta 2.0** is out. The update to Riley Testut's game emulator for iPhone and iPad launched on October 22, according to [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/games/1016524/delta-2-version-ios-duo-gaming-emulator-update) and Delta's own [release notes](https://faq.deltaemulator.com/release-notes).

Delta is an **emulator**, not an official Nintendo or Sega product. It doesn't come with games, so you supply your own game files.

## iPhone Duo support

Testut told The Verge that Delta 2.0 optimizes controller skins for "every pose in both portrait and landscape" on Apple's foldable **iPhone Duo**, and that split-view multitasking still works. Folding the Duo puts an emulated DS game to sleep, the same way closing a real DS does.

## Also new

- **Sega Genesis** support, with new skins modeled on the classic Genesis controller
- **Pokémon DLC:** custom in-game experiences such as event items and rare Pokémon for mainline Game Boy Advance Pokémon games. Testut says the community-made DLC was built without AI tools and the first pack is coming "soon."
- **Experimental:** support for Epilogue's GB and SN Operator cartridge readers, plus custom screen filters such as a CRT look
- A **Liquid Glass** redesign, animated controller skins (third-party skins supported), and new Recents and Favorites pages

## Now on the EU App Store

The Verge says this is Delta's first release on the **EU App Store**, alongside AltStore PAL. It also adds an optional "Become a Patron" in-app purchase.
