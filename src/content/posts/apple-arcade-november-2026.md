Apple Arcade has five new games on the way in November, according to [GamingonPhone](https://gamingonphone.com/news/apple-arcade-all-upcoming-games-for-november-2026/). We checked each one against Apple's pages or the developer's.

## November 5

- **NBA 2K27 Arcade Edition** (2K). The [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/nba-2k27-arcade-edition/id6760640090) shows "Expected Nov 5, 2026" on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV. It adds all **15 WNBA teams and 180 active WNBA players** for Quick Match and Blacktop. MyCAREER now starts your player at 80 overall and covers 12 key matchups.
- **Crossy Road Rush** (Hipster Whale). The [official site](https://crossyroad.com/rush) confirms November 5 and says it's exclusive to Apple Arcade. It's a platforming runner with 50-plus levels in Adventure Mode, a daily Survival Mode, and up to four players in local co-op and online.
- **KAMI 2+** (State of Play), a color-fill puzzle game.
- **Food Stylist: Design Game+** (Zeste), a food-presentation sim.

[MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/06/crossy-road-rush-apple-arcade/) reported Apple's October 6 announcement of Crossy Road Rush along with KAMI 2 and Food Stylist on November 5.

## November 10

- **Football Manager 27 Touch**, according to GamingonPhone. We couldn't find an Apple page confirming the date yet, so treat November 10 as reported.

## What we couldn't confirm

Apple's [Apple Arcade newsroom page](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/topics/apple-arcade/) doesn't yet have a November roundup post. The NBA 2K27 and Crossy Road Rush dates come from Apple's App Store and the developer. The KAMI 2+ and Food Stylist+ dates come from Apple's announcement as reported by MacRumors. The Football Manager date comes only from GamingonPhone.
